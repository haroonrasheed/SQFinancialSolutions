What We Offer

Comprehensive Financial & Business Solutions

Eight core service lines designed to support your business at every stage — from formation to long-term strategic growth.

Business Registration Services

End-to-end support for setting up your business the right way — from initial structuring to regulatory registration — so you can start operating with full legal compliance.

  • Company incorporation & SECP registration
  • Sole proprietorship & partnership registration
  • NTN & sales tax registration
  • Chamber of Commerce & trade body registration
  • Trademark & brand registration support
  • Sector-specific regulatory licensing

Audit & Assurance Services

Independent, thorough, and accurate audits that give stakeholders confidence in your financial reporting and internal controls.

  • Statutory & external audits
  • Internal audit & controls review
  • Financial statement review
  • Special-purpose & compliance audits
  • Risk-based audit methodology
  • IFRS & ISA-aligned reporting

Human Resource (HR) Services

Practical HR frameworks and workforce support that help you build, manage, and retain a compliant, productive team.

  • HR policy design & employee handbooks
  • Recruitment & staffing support
  • Payroll management
  • Performance management systems
  • Labor law compliance
  • Organizational structuring

Taxation Services

Strategic tax planning and full compliance support that keeps you on the right side of the law while minimizing your tax burden.

  • Income tax return filing (individuals & corporates)
  • Sales tax & withholding tax compliance
  • Tax planning & advisory
  • FBR representation & correspondence
  • Tax audits & appeals
  • International taxation advisory

Business Advisory Services

Strategic guidance grounded in real-world experience, helping you make confident decisions at every stage of growth.

  • Business plans & feasibility studies
  • Financial modeling & forecasting
  • Restructuring & turnaround advisory
  • Mergers & acquisitions support
  • Startup advisory
  • Growth strategy consulting

Information Technology Audit

Assessing IT systems, controls, and cybersecurity posture to protect data integrity and support regulatory compliance.

  • IT general controls review
  • Cybersecurity risk assessment
  • Systems & process audits
  • Data integrity & governance review
  • ERP / software implementation audits
  • IT compliance advisory

Accounting Software Services

Implementation and support for modern accounting systems that streamline your financial operations and reporting.

  • Accounting software selection & implementation
  • ERP customization & support
  • Bookkeeping automation
  • Staff training on financial systems
  • Cloud accounting migration
  • Ongoing technical support

Retainership Services

Ongoing, dedicated financial and advisory support so you always have expert guidance on hand — without the overhead of an in-house team.

  • Dedicated monthly advisory support
  • Ongoing compliance monitoring
  • Periodic financial reporting
  • Priority access to our expert team
  • Cost-effective long-term partnership
  • Customized retainer packages
